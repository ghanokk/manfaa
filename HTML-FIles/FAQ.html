Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I create an account?

Click “Sign up”, enter your details, and confirm your email address.

Can I change my password?

Yes — go to your profile → Settings → Change Password.

Are the courses free?

Some courses are free, while others are paid. Check each course description.

How do I contact support?

You can use the contact form or email us at support@example.com.

Do you provide certificates?

Yes — many courses award certificates upon completion.

What browsers are supported?

Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari — for best results, use the latest version.
